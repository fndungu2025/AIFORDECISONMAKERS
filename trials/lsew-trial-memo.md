# Trial memo: Lone Star Exhibit Works (LSEW)

> **HYPOTHETICAL.** LSEW does not exist; every fact below was invented to test the GES M&A agent team.
> All multiples are unverified assumptions (no sourced comps). Not investment, legal, tax, or accounting advice.

## Fact pack (invented)
Dallas-Fort Worth fabricator, founder-owned (age 61, no successor), broker process, ask $45M. FY2025 revenue $48M (fab 55%, rentals 20%, install labor 25%); reported EBITDA $6.2M; ~140 Teamsters members, multi-employer pension, CBA expires ~20 months; 3 official-contractor shows = 18% of revenue ($8.6M), expire ~14 months, one with a change-of-control consent clause; top customer 22%, top 5 = 46%; biennial show 9% of revenue in odd years (FY2025 is odd); plant leased from founder below market; deferred revenue/deposits $5.1M; capex ~3.5% of revenue.

## Recommendation
**Proceed to diligence only, at a much lower price and with conditions. Do not pay the $45M ask.**

## Enterprise value (assumed 4.5-5.5x, unverified)
Low 25.4 (5.65 x 4.5) | Mid 29.75 (5.95 x 5.0) | High 34.4 (6.25 x 5.5).
Capex cross-check: mid EBITDA less capex (~1.60) = 4.35, so $29.75M is ~6.8x, which is rich for a capex-heavy fabricator. Stay at or below mid.
If deposit cash is swept at close, treat the $5.1M as debt-like: mid EV falls to ~24.65.

## What decides the deal
1. **Contract cliff:** $8.6M of revenue in 3 contracts expiring together; an acquisition invites a rebid, and organizers can extract concessions for consent. Model price cuts, not only loss.
2. **Pension:** withdrawal liability unknown and potentially material. Get the plan's estimate (~2-4 weeks) and an actuary; treat as a dollar-for-dollar price reduction.
3. **Earnings quality:** odd-year show and unverified add-backs; rent needs an appraisal.
4. **Concentration:** top customer 22% (loss scenario ~ $16M of value; do not add to contract risk, they overlap).

## Conditions
1. Price off even-year EBITDA with market rent; treat pension (and deposits if swept) as deductions.
2. Organizer consent as a closing condition, or earnout/escrow tied to renewal of the 3 contracts.
3. Founder stays, signs a non-compete, and leases the plant at market rent.
4. No workforce combination before the CBA is settled.
5. **Walk away** if the pension liability is large relative to price, or if 2+ of the 3 shows won't consent, or price exceeds ~$30M on current information.

## Cheapest next steps
Plan pension estimate; broker/seller sounding of the consent-clause organizer after an overlap/antitrust check; limited QoE (rent, owner pay, legal add-back, odd/even effect); sourced comps; revenue cube by customer/show/year.

## Open issues
GES ownership and funding capacity were never confirmed (buyer assumptions unverified). Whether $45M ask is revenue- or EBITDA-based is unknown. Overlap between GES and LSEW shows/plants is unknown.

## Process notes
Agents run: industry-strategist, deal-sourcing, valuation-analyst (twice), financial-diligence, contract-organizer-analyst, labor-specialist, red-team. The first valuation used the full claimed $7.2M EBITDA while diligence put it at $6.3-6.9M; the inconsistency was caught during synthesis and the valuation rerun on the diligence view. Red Team verdict: **Proceed with conditions** (diligence only, lower price).
